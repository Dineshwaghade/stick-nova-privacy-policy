Stick Nova — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Stick Nova has no accounts and no sign-in, and it never asks who you are. Your puzzle progress is stored on your own phone and is not sent anywhere. The only data that leaves your device is what Google's advertising service collects in order to show ads, and you are asked about that separately.

What the game stores on your phone

  • Which levels you have solved, and the stars you earned for them.
  • Your hint count, your daily-puzzle streak, and which mode you played last.
  • Counts of puzzles solved and sticks moved, shown on the Stats screen.
  • Your sound, vibration and tutorial settings.

All of this is kept in the app's own private storage on your device. It is not uploaded, not shared, and never seen by us. Uninstalling the game deletes it. You can also erase it at any time from Settings → Reset progress inside the game.

Advertising

Stick Nova is free and is paid for by ads served by Google AdMob. There are three kinds, and this is all of them:

KindWhere it appears
Banner A strip at the bottom of the main menu only. There is never a banner on a puzzle screen.
Full screen Occasionally, only after you tap to move on from a puzzle you have already solved. Never during a puzzle, and never when the app opens.
Rewarded video Only if you choose to tap a button offering hints. It always says what it gives you before you tap it, and it never plays on its own.

To show those ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier, device and app information, and approximate location derived from your IP address. Google uses this to serve and measure ads, and to detect fraud. How Google handles that information is described here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

We do not receive any of that information ourselves, and we do not sell personal information.

Your choices about ads

  • If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown the first time the game runs. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy options inside the game.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID from your phone's own settings, usually under Settings → Google → Ads.
  • You never have to watch a rewarded video. Everything it offers can also be reached without one, and undo and reset are always free.

Permissions

The game itself asks for nothing. Every permission it declares comes from the Google Mobile Ads library it uses:

PermissionWhy it is there
INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEFetching ads.
AD_IDThe advertising identifier described above.
ACCESS_ADSERVICES_*Android's Privacy Sandbox, declared by the ads library.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE, WAKE_LOCKDeclared by a library the ads SDK depends on. The game does not use them.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for an account, an email address, or any personal details.
  • It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • It has no chat, no user-generated content, and no social features.
  • The puzzles are built into the app, so it plays with no connection at all.

Children

Stick Nova is not directed to children under 13, and is not part of Google Play's Designed for Families programme. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through this app, please contact us at the address below and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date at the top. The game links to this page directly, so it always shows the current version.
